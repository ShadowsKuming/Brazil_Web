## Our First Meeting

The UK and Brazilian research teams met together for the first time, marking a major milestone in the HAII Lab Brazil collaboration. Researchers from Newcastle University and UFRGS gathered to align on project goals, discuss research directions, and begin building the cross-national partnerships that will shape our work going forward.

This meeting brought together project leads, PhD students, and Brazilian student collaborators across all five research groups, setting the stage for an exciting programme of inclusive AI research for children in Brazil.
