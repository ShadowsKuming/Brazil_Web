## Bio

Arthur Marques de Oliveira is a PhD student in Informatics in Education and Language Studies at the Federal University of Rio Grande do Sul (UFRGS), Brazil, and a professor in Technology courses at Centro Universitário CESUCA. He holds a Master's degree in Language Studies from UFRGS. His work takes a transdisciplinary approach, sitting at the intersection of technology, identity, and education.

ORCiD: [0000-0001-9755-4277](https://orcid.org/0000-0001-9755-4277)

### Research Interests
- Artificial Intelligence in Education
- AI co-intelligence
- Teacher education and educational technologies
- Distance education and Digital Educational Materials
- Sociolinguistics and Queer Linguistics
- Identities, language practices, and diversity
