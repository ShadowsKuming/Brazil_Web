## Bio

Xin Shu is a PhD researcher in Human–Computer Interaction at Newcastle University. His research focuses on designing AI-powered, multimodal learning systems that support understanding, creativity, and emotional engagement, particularly in educational contexts. He is especially interested in how large language models and embodied interaction can be integrated into learning tools to make complex knowledge more accessible and explainable. His recent work explores AI-supported music learning, affective interaction, and inclusive educational technologies.

### Research interests
- Human–Computer Interaction (HCI)
- AI for Education
- Agent-based System
- Multimodal Interaction
- Learning Technologies
- Music and Creative Learning Systems
