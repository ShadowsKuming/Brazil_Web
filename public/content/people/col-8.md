## Bio

Vivian Meurer Lameira is a joint PhD candidate (cotutelle) in Language Studies, with a focus on Lexical Studies and Translation, at the Graduate Program in Languages (Letras) of the Federal University of Rio Grande do Sul (UFRGS), and a PhD candidate in Education at Concordia University, Canada. She holds a Master's in Language Studies from UFRGS and Bachelor's degrees in Languages (Letras) from UFRGS and in Pedagogy from the University of Caxias do Sul (UCS).

### Research Interests
- Corpus Linguistics
- English for Academic Purposes (EAP)
- AI-supported academic writing
- Lexical Studies and Translation
