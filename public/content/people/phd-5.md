## Bio

Marci Ma is a UK-based HCI researcher and designer focused on dismantling digital exclusion through tangible interaction and "contestable" AI frameworks. Her research investigates how physical interfaces and generative models can scaffold learners' art appreciation and cultural interpretation in resource-constrained environments. By prioritising cross-cultural co-design, she develops systems that challenge dominant Western narrative tropes and empower marginalised voices to lead the interpretive process. Her interdisciplinary background as a curator and educator informs her commitment to building pluralistic digital experiences that value local heritage over hegemonic canons.

### Research interests
- Tangible & Embodied Interaction
- Human-AI Co-Creation
- Educational Equity & Accessibility
- Critical Computing & Algorithmic Bias
- Cross-Cultural Co-Design
