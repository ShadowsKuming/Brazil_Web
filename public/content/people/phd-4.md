## Bio

I am a second year PhD candidate whose research focuses on the intersection of Serious Games and Game Interaction, with a specific interest in Edu-Games. I hold an MSc in Human-Computer Interaction from the University of Nottingham, where I developed a deep interest in interactive systems. Prior to my PhD, I held short-term roles in game design and product management, which provided me with a unique industry lens. When I'm not in the lab, I am an avid gamer, often finding research inspiration while playing video games in my spare time.

### Research interests
- Serious Games
- Edu-Games
- Game Interaction
- Remote Learning
- Crowdsourced Learning
