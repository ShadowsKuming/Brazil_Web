## Bio

I am a PhD candidate at Newcastle University with a research focus on generative models and their practical and theoretical foundations. My work primarily explores GANs and diffusion models, including model architecture, training stability, evaluation, and system-level design. With a background in computer science and strong experience in deep learning frameworks, I am interested in building scalable and reusable AI training pipelines that bridge research ideas and real-world applications. More broadly, I explore agent-based AI systems and multimodal generation, aiming to design robust AI systems that integrate model understanding, engineering practice, and applied use cases.

### Research interests
- Generative Models
- GANs
- Diffusion Models
- Multimodal Learning
- AI Systems and Training Pipelines
- Agent-based AI Workflows
