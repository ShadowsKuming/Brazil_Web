## Bio

Ana Eliza Pereira Bocorny is Associate Professor of Applied Linguistics at the Federal University of Rio Grande do Sul (UFRGS), Brazil. She holds a PhD in Language Studies and completed a CAPES-funded postdoc at Northern Arizona University. Since 2021, she coordinates the undergraduate distance English programme at UFRGS.

### Research Interests
- Corpus Linguistics
- English for Academic Purposes (EAP)
- AI-supported academic writing
